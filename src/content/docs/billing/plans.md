ReleaseLoop is sold as a **lifetime deal**: **$49 per seat**, paid once. You choose how many seats your team needs, and every seat gets every feature. There is no monthly bill.

Every new workspace starts with **14 days of full access**, no credit card needed. When the trial ends, the workspace owner buys lifetime seats from the **Billing** page.

## What a lifetime seat includes

| | Lifetime access |
|---|---|
| **Price** | $49 per seat, one-time |
| **Seats** | As many as you buy (up to 50 per purchase) |
| **Releases** | Unlimited |
| **Artists** | Unlimited |
| **Contacts** | Unlimited |
| **EPKs** | Unlimited |
| **Storage** | 1 GB per seat |

One seat is one team member. A solo artist needs 1 seat ($49). A label with three people needs 3 seats ($147).

## Every feature, on every seat

- Release management (tracks, statuses, metadata, UPCs, ISRCs)
- Task management with assignments, due dates, and subtask checklists
- Calendar view
- Budget and expense tracking
- Activity history
- Marketing planner
- Electronic Press Kits (EPKs)
- **Release templates**, so every single follows the same proven plan
- **Artist statements**, unlimited, with custom statement branding
- **Royalty CSV imports** from DistroKid, TuneCore, CD Baby, or any other distributor
- **Payout statements**, so you can settle with your artists on whatever schedule you agree to
- **Google Drive integration** for master WAVs, artwork, and promo assets

## Adding seats later

Your team grew? Buy more seats from the **Billing** page at any time for $49 each. Your data, settings, and team stay exactly as they are; only your seat count changes. See [Managing Your Billing](/billing/subscription/) and [Team Members & Roles](/team/members/).

## Older tiered lifetime plans

Workspaces that bought one of the earlier tiered lifetime plans keep exactly what they paid for. These plans are no longer sold to new workspaces.

| | Solo Lifetime | Label Lifetime | Agency / Pro Lifetime |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Seats** | 1 | 3 | 10 |
| **Releases** | 15 | 150 | 1,000 |
| **Artists** | 5 | 75 | 250 |
| **Contacts** | 100 | 1,000 | Unlimited |
| **EPKs** | 5 | 75 | Unlimited |
| **Storage** | 1 GB | 25 GB | 100 GB |

On these plans, Solo includes 3 artist statements per quarter; Label adds unlimited statements, branding, templates, royalty imports, and Google Drive; Agency / Pro adds payout statements and priority support. Owners of a tiered plan can still move up a tier by paying the difference, or buy extra seats, from the **Billing** page.
