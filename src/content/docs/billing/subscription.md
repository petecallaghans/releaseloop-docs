Billing is handled through the **Billing** page, accessible from the sidebar. ReleaseLoop is a [lifetime deal](/billing/plans/) at $49 per seat: you pay once, so there is no recurring subscription to maintain.

## Viewing your plan and usage

The Billing page shows:

- Your current plan and status, or how many days of your 14-day full access trial remain
- Usage against your limits: releases, artists, contacts, storage, and team seats
- The seat picker for buying lifetime seats or adding more

## Buying lifetime access

When your trial ends, or any time before, the workspace owner can buy lifetime access in about 30 seconds:

1. Go to the **Billing** page
2. In the **Lifetime access: $49 per seat** panel, choose how many seats you need. The picker starts at your current team size and shows the total as you change it
3. Click **Get lifetime access**. You are redirected to Stripe's secure checkout
4. Complete the one-time payment
5. Your workspace switches to lifetime access as soon as Stripe confirms the payment, with every feature unlocked

If your workspace has more members than the seats you buy, everyone keeps access, but you need a seat for each person before you can invite anyone new.

If your workspace was on an older monthly subscription, buying lifetime seats cancels that subscription automatically, so you are not charged again.

## Adding team seats

Lifetime workspaces can buy extra seats at any time:

1. On the **Billing** page, find the **Add seats** panel
2. Choose a quantity. The panel shows the total price and your new seat limit
3. Click **Buy seats** and complete Stripe checkout
4. Your seat limit increases as soon as payment is confirmed

See [Team Members & Roles](/team/members/) for how seats and roles work.

## Who can manage billing

Only the workspace **Owner** can make purchases. Your A&R and marketing team members will not accidentally change your plan or see payment details.

## Older tiered lifetime plans

If your workspace is on an older Solo, Label, or Agency / Pro lifetime plan, the Billing page still lets you move up a tier by paying the difference, or add seats. See [Plans & Pricing](/billing/plans/#older-tiered-lifetime-plans) for those plans' limits. If you reach a limit on one of these plans, your existing data is never deleted or locked; you just cannot create new items beyond the limit until you upgrade.

## Legacy monthly subscriptions

If your workspace is on an older monthly or annual subscription, click **Manage subscription** on the Billing page to open the Stripe customer portal, where you can update your payment method, view invoices, or cancel. If you cancel, you keep access until the end of the billing period and your data is preserved: release metadata, artist profiles, contacts, and financials all stay intact. You can also switch to lifetime access by buying seats, as described above.
